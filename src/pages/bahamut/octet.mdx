## General Breakdown
Grand Octet brings the 8 dragons - Twin, Nael and Bahamut, as well as the 5 divebomb dragons - back for the final mechanic of the phase before Adds!

### The Dives
Every dragon will dive in this mechanic, baited and diving in the following order:  
- Nael
- The 5 divebomb dragons
- Bahamut
- Twin (After towers spawn)

Nael will snapshot first, then the divebomb markers will one by one as they disappear, followed by Bahamut when it appears and lastly Twin when it appears while the towers are out.
The 5 divebomb dragons dive in order CW/CCW of Bahamut depending on if he spawns on a Cardinal (CCW) or intercardinal (CW).  
Players must strategically bait the dragons one by one in ways that do not cause the baits to hit the party, nor leave no room for the next baiter.

There's no ordering to who gets what bait, so watch your head as each bait comes out, the only rule is that you wont get 2 dives providing 8/8 players were alive by the end of the Grand Octet castbar.

### The Towers, Megaflare Stack & Twintania's Dive
After Bahamut has been baited and dives, 4 towers will spawn at an equal distance from the middle of the room. At the same time 4 players will be marked with the Megaflare stack ring.  
During this, the player who has not yet baited a dive should position accordingly to bait Twin as the twin snapshot will shortly follow all these entities spawning, then the players must resolve the megaflare stack and the towers within short succession. 
The megaflare stack will go off first, then the towers and Twin dive moments after.

The way this is handled is that Twin should be pointed away from the middle of the room, then the stack markers should stack together either outside of the 4 towers, or inside one of them with some shields and light party mit.  
While this is happening then the remaining non-stack players can spread out into any towers that do not have a body in. Wait for twin's Twisting dive snapshot and move to another point inside your tower or inside the arena to dodge the twister that comes with.  
Twin's dive and the twisters will snapshot at the same time as the towers do, so make sure you don't leave your tower for the twister. You don't need to do laps around your tower either as this will lead to you walking back into your own twister more often than not.

#### Tank LB
If the party has LB2 or LB3 the mechanic becomes much more trivial at the end. Have your shield healer shield the party and place soil/use kerachole and you can very easily resolve the towers/stack without much thought.  

**LB2**:
- Make sure Twin is still baited properly.
- Megaflare rings find a buddy, squishy will survive a 2-stack and can even take a tower after.
- Make sure all 4 towers have at least 1 body in them.

**LB3**:
- Ignore the twin bait, pop Arms Length/Surecast to eat the knockback.
- Ignore the stack, just ensure all 4 towers have a body in them.
