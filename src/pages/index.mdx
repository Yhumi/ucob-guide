## Welcome
Welcome to an in-depth guide on UCoB (cob) - The Unending Coil of Bahamut (Ultimate) - written by a collection of serial Cob Enjoyers passionate about teaching/helping people to clear the fight!  
While this guide will go in depth generically on the mechanics of the fight, it is written by people who primarily use NA pf strats. 
As such, there are sections in this guide labelled as NA PF Specific sections. These sections are not necessary to learn the fight, but are useful if you're using the NAUR strats!

### The Golden rule
While you are progging/reclearing this fight **PLEASE** consider everything recoverable until it isn't.  
UCoB has a lot of recoverable situations, and it isn't uncommon to see clears of this fight with the total death count of 15+. If you have players alive who can raise **the pull can still continue.**  
Do not throw away your chance of progression because you feel like its over in the moment, let the pull end with a real wipe to mechs (unless there's no raises alive).

This also means that your most valuable casters are RDM and then SMN. Consider bringing these jobs over PCT/BLM in prog. The DPS check in UCoB is very lenient so focus on getting through the mechs first.  
Oh, and don't forget your Super-Ethers!

If you wish to read further about ways to recover in UCoB, [read the fantastic recovery guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AqE5r_k0iwLOnnyXQWF53FhSeWVyNPhkQVCDaacRo1g/edit?usp=sharing) or check the recovery sections on this guide for specific mechanics!
